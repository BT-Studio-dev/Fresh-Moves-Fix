## Bottom Cyclops Eye

**The Magenta areas 🟪** are pixels that only exist in the **Wide Player Model** (the one with 4-pixel-wide arms). In the **Slim Player Model** (the one with 3-pixel-wide arms), those **Magenta areas 🟪** will appear transparent, so keep that in mind while editing your skin.
