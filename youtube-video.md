# YouTube video about the website – plan and script

A short video (about 2 minutes) that shows what https://fresh-moves-fix.vercel.app/ can do.
You record the screen; the text below tells you what to show and what to say.

## How to record

1. Open the website in Chrome and make the window full screen (F11).
2. Record the screen:
   - Windows: press **Win + Alt + R** to start and stop (Xbox Game Bar). The video goes to *Videos > Captures*.
   - Or use the free program **OBS Studio** ("Display Capture", then "Start Recording").
3. Record each shot below as its own short clip. Move the mouse slowly.
4. Put the clips in a row in a free editor (Clipchamp on Windows, or CapCut) and add the voice or text.
5. Export as 1080p and upload it on https://studio.youtube.com (Create > Upload videos).

## Description (copy and paste)

```
Fresh Moves Fix is a free resource pack with smoother player animations and animated eyes
for Minecraft 1.20 to 26.3. This video shows the website that comes with it.

Website: https://fresh-moves-fix.vercel.app/
Download the pack: https://github.com/BT-Studio-dev/Fresh-Moves-Fix/releases

On the website you can:
• Skin eye editor – add blinking, moving eyes to your skin
• Skin Painter 3D – paint your skin right on a 3D model
• Minecraft 3D Model – download your player as an animated .gltf file
• Minecraft Animation – put movements in a row and save a video
• Minecraft Title – make a 3D title in Blockbench

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

The pack needs the mods Entity Model Features (EMF) and Entity Texture Features (ETF).
Fresh Moves Fix is a fork of Fresh Moves by IthanMendoza. The pack is still in beta.
```
