Fresh Moves Fix - player animation resource pack

REQUIRED MODS
You need the LATEST versions of these two mods to use this pack.
They are client-side mods, available for Fabric, Forge and NeoForge.

  Entity Model Features (EMF)   v2.0.2 or newer
  https://modrinth.com/mod/entity-model-features

  Entity Texture Features (ETF) v6.0.1 or newer
  https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures

On Minecraft 1.21.9 and newer use EMF 3.0.12 or newer, otherwise armour
does not follow the animations.

On Minecraft 26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2 use EMF 3.3.2 or newer (older
versions do not start or crash there). On 26.3 use EMF 3.3.6 or newer.
EMF for 26.x exists for Fabric and NeoForge.

INSTALL
1. Install EMF and ETF.
2. Put this zip (do not unzip it) in the "resourcepacks" folder.
3. In Minecraft: Options > Resource Packs, move the pack to the right, Done.

EMOTES
Stand still and look straight up. Every tap on sneak is the next emote:
1 wave, 2 cheer, 3 clap, 4 dab, 5 dance, 6 jumping jacks, 7 T-pose.
Look away or walk to stop.

TALKING MOUTH (skins with a mouth from the website eye editor)
Look forward and tap sneak twice quickly: the mouth moves as if talking.
Double tap again to stop. It does not follow your real voice.

SIT EMOTE
Stand still, sneak and look straight down: the player sits on the ground.
Look up again or let go of sneak to stand up.

Based on Fresh Moves by IthanMendoza (CC0).
