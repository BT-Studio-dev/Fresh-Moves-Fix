# Skin Eye Editor

The Skin Eye Editor helps you add Fresh Moves Fix eye markers to a Minecraft Java skin. Your skin stays in your browser until you download it.

## Start with your skin

1. Open the **Skin eye editor** and upload a 64×64 skin PNG, or pick a default skin.
2. The editor checks for supported eye markers and tries to recognize ordinary eye pixels. Automatic detection is an estimate: unusual skins may need manual adjustment. If the editor cannot make a confident guess, it keeps the original face instead of inventing a look.
3. Check the **Eye shape**, **Position** and **Eyebrows** controls. Try the thumbnails in the dropdowns and adjust them if needed. Eyebrow positions that would overlap the selected eyes are unavailable; move the eyes lower to use those positions. Changing eye position can reset an incompatible eyebrow choice to Do not change.

## Choose your look

Use the Eyes card for shape, position, eyebrows, mouth and compatible eyelashes. Use the Colours card to change colours. The Face and 3D previews update as you make changes. Eyebrows and eyelashes in these previews are approximations; their in-game appearance may differ.

Use the format controls for Steve (wide) or Alex (slim) arms, then download your finished PNG. The background colour of the 3D preview is not saved into the skin.

## Use the skin in Minecraft

Apply the downloaded PNG to your Java Edition skin. Install one compatible Fresh Moves Fix resource pack and matching EMF and ETF versions. Test your look in-game; some mouth and eyebrow animations have not been independently verified.

## Blockbench and expressive faces

The video [Make expressive faces in Blockbench](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hTFKQrj8ZV4) by notsparkskye demonstrates building a face rig for posed renders. It is a useful reference for designing expressions, but a Blockbench rig is not the same thing as a Fresh Moves Fix skin or resource pack.

For Fresh Moves Fix, keep the normal face on the skin and add the pack's marker pixels. The editor exports a skin PNG, not a Blockbench model or animation. The **Simulate blinking and eye movement** checkbox offers a simplified website-only preview; it starts off so you can inspect the eyes clearly. Check the result in Minecraft with a compatible pack and EMF/ETF. The website does not import or use the video's rig.
